About Us

Your Trusted Partner in Construction & Engineering

A Liberian-owned firm built on professionalism, safety, and excellence.

Wreyou Construction Company (WCC) is a proudly Liberian-owned construction and engineering company established and incorporated under the laws of the Republic of Liberia in 2016. Since our founding, we have remained committed to delivering high-quality construction, civil engineering, and architectural design solutions that meet the highest standards of professionalism, safety, and excellence. With a team of experienced engineers, architects, project managers, and skilled professionals, we bring innovative ideas, technical expertise, and practical solutions to every project.

We combine modern construction practices with innovative engineering solutions to create structures that are safe, sustainable, and built to last. At WCC, we believe that every successful project begins with strong partnerships. Through transparent communication, careful planning, and attention to detail, we transform our clients’ visions into reality while maintaining an unwavering commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To bring innovation, creativity, professionalism, and excellence to the road construction and civil engineering industry, while conforming to the principles and quality management requirements of ISO 9001. We are committed to delivering safe, durable, cost-effective, and sustainable infrastructure through modern construction practices, skilled professionals, quality materials, and effective project management — continuously striving to exceed client expectations and contribute to the sustainable development of communities throughout Liberia’s 15 political subdivisions.

Our Vision

To position Wreyou Construction Company as a leading engineering and construction firm in Liberia, recognized for quality, innovation, professionalism, and exceptional project delivery. We aspire to design and build safe, durable, and sustainable infrastructure that creates extraordinary value for our customers, clients, partners, and communities — while creating meaningful career opportunities for Liberia’s youth and building a strong, motivated workforce.
