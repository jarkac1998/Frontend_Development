About Me

A little about who I am and how I work.

I’m an IT professional from Monrovia, Liberia, with hands-on experience across the full spectrum of modern technology work — from building websites to administering databases to designing brand visuals. I enjoy translating a client’s idea into something functional, fast, and good-looking.

My background spans web development, frontend development, database administration, graphic design, website management, and digital solutions for businesses and institutions. I like projects that need more than one skill set — the kind where I design the interface, build it, and make sure the data behind it is organized and secure.

Whether you need a new website, an upgrade to an existing system, a properly structured database, or visual materials for your brand, I bring a calm, detail-driven approach from the first conversation to the final handover.
